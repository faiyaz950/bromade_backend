You can delete your {{ brand }} account and personal data at any time.

From the app

  1. Open the {{ brand }} app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is closed immediately and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email {{ support_email }} from the email address on your account, or include the mobile number you signed up with. We will confirm it is your account and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, email address and Google sign-in link
  • Saved addresses and map pins
  • Support chats
  • Before/after visit photos
  • Push-notification tokens and referral code

What we keep

A record of past bookings and payments (service, date, amount) with your personal details removed, because tax and accounting laws require it. It can no longer be linked to you.

Before you delete

If you have an upcoming booking, cancel it or wait until the visit is done first. Unused coupons and referral rewards are lost when the account is deleted.
